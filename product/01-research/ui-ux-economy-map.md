# How the UI/UX economy works — 21 Oct 2026

## Who ultimately pays
Businesses that build digital products. They pay for design because it moves revenue (conversion, retention),
cuts build cost (reuse, fewer rebuilds) and reduces risk (accessibility law, brand consistency).
Large enterprises = ~65% of UX services spend; finance is the biggest industry buyer (Grand View Research).

## Where the money flows (largest → smallest, roughly)
| Pool | Who earns | Size / signal | 2026 trend |
|---|---|---|---|
| Salaries | In-house designers, DS teams | Largest pool | Senior/generalist recovering, entry level scarce (NN/g); DS teams understaffed (61%, zeroheight) |
| Services | Agencies, consultancies, freelancers | UX services ~$8.3B in 2026 (Grand View, analyst estimate) | Strategy/consulting growing; enterprises dominate |
| Tools / platforms | Figma, Framer, Webflow, Storybook, zeroheight, Supernova, Tokens Studio | Figma alone ~$1.42B 2026 guidance, ~690K paid customers, 1,525 at >$100K ARR | Platforms absorb features (AI, MCP, Guidelines); AI credits = new revenue |
| Assets | UI kits, templates, icons, plugins | Untitled UI, DSS ($19/mo), Figma Community (15% fee; **not approving new paid-file sellers**) | Commoditised by free kits + AI generation |
| Education | Bootcamps, courses, certifications | Brad Frost AI+DS $995; Designlab AI cert | Entry-level career-changer demand weak; upskilling seniors in AI growing |
| Attention | LinkedIn, newsletters, YouTube, podcasts | Sponsorships; mainly a funnel into the pools above | Where trust and buyers are found |

## The 2026 shift
- AI and design systems make producing UI cheaper → screens are less of a differentiator (NN/g)
- Value moves to: systems, strategy, judgment, trust, accessibility, AI integration
- Platforms (Figma) capture more of the tool layer each year
- Buyers pay for outcomes (adoption, speed, compliance), not artifacts

## Implications for us
- Selling artifacts (kits, generators, templates) = shrinking margin, platform risk
- Selling outcomes to companies (services → productised services → tools) = where budgets are
- Attention (Ali's LinkedIn) is the acquisition channel, not the product
- Education works for upskilling working designers, but needs proof/audience first
