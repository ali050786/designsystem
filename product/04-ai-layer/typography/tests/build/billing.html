Questions

When am I charged?

On the same day each month as your first payment. If that day doesn't exist in a month, we charge on the last day.

Can I get a refund for unused seats?

Seats you remove are credited to your next invoice, to the day. Credits appear as a minus amount in the table above.

Where do invoices go?

To the billing contact in your workspace settings. Every invoice is also here, and you can download it as a PDF.
