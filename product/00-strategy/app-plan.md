# ds app — plan (Phase 2, Next.js)
Agreed with Ali 24 Oct 2026. Working name `ds`. Hosting: Vercel free tier. Emails: form to a Google Sheet.

## Rules
- Guides, handoffs, rulebooks and tokens stay generated by the engine; the app reads them at build time and never rewrites them.
- The engine is imported as-is (ES modules, no dependencies) for the live generator.
- Every page of the app passes our own eight checkers and eight audits, in CI.
- No decorative numbering in pages or navigation.

## Site map
- Home: what ds is, the accessibility promise, try the generator, take the self-check.
- Foundations: one page per foundation (colour, typography, spacing, layout, radius, border, elevation, motion), built from its GUIDE.md, with the demo as live React examples and a switch bar (theme, density, radius style, motion, 200% text).
- Generate yours: brand colour and the few config choices in, live preview of every foundation, check results, downloads (tokens JSON, CSS, Tailwind preset, Figma JSON, AI rulebooks).
- Self-check: 15 questions, a score card, email form to a Google Sheet, "book an audit".
- For AI: the rulebooks, contracts and prompts.
- Changelog: versions from day one.

## Order of work
App skeleton with tokens.css and the shell; foundation pages; generator; self-check and email; checks in CI; GitHub repo and Vercel (Ali's accounts).
