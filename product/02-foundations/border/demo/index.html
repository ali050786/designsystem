The current item

Joined parts

Parts that touch share one line: .ds-join overlaps them by one control border and lifts the part that changes, so its whole border and its focus ring show.

Focus rings

Press Tab. The ring is an outline outside the element — never a border, never a box-shadow. A full-width row has no room around it, so its ring is drawn inside with a negative offset: still an outline, so it survives forced colours.
