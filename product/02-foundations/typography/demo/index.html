Sharing a project

Invite people by email, or share a link that only your team can open.

Open the project, choose Share, then copy the link. Anyone in your workspace can open it; people outside need an invitation. To limit the link to one team, run ds share --team design before you send it.

Links stop working when you remove someone from the project, so you never have to chase old copies. You can see who opened a link, and when, under Activity.

  • Invitations expire after 14 days.
  • Guests can comment but can't change settings.
  • Owners can move a project to another workspace.
